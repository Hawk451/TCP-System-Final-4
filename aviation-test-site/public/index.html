About SkyForce

Flying South Africa Forward

Founded in 2008, SkyForce Aviation has grown from a small Rand Airport operation into South Africa's most trusted aircraft sales and services company. We are based at Lanseria International Airport, Johannesburg, with service centres in Cape Town and Durban.

Our team of 45 aviation professionals includes SACAA-licensed engineers, commercial pilots, and finance specialists — all committed to putting more South Africans in the air safely and affordably.
